Rules

Tavlatus

Score, then survive the blind.

Dice roll automatically. Select a white checker, move by an available die, then press Win after beating the target to visit the shop.

Base move
Every moved checker scores 10 Chips x die value before Mult.
Scoring
Move Score = Chips x global Mult, then checker and tile Mult can push it higher.
Pass-over hooks
Checkers now jump pip-by-pip. Passed pips can trigger future effects before the checker lands.
Break
Land on one red checker to break it for +200 Chips.
Allied landing
Land on white checkers to gain +50 Chips for each white checker already there.
Bear off
Move past pip 24 to score +250 Chips.
Doubles
Rolling a double turns the matched dice into four moves of that value.
Payout
Clearing a normal blind pays 3 Akçe, the boss pays 5 Akçe, and unused rolls pay 1 Akçe each.
Mars
Win before any moving enemy reaches the end to double the payout.
Enemies
Every enemy phase, the two enemy pieces farthest from the player base move one by one. Pawns move 2 pips, or 1 pip when blocked by a defended white gate. Rams can attack a defended gate and push one white checker to the bar.
Tiles
Relic-pips are placed in your bottom deck. Forge gives that checker +1 Mult forever, Market doubles Chips for that move, and other relic-pips add break, money, haste, or Mult effects.
Shop
Clear a blind to visit the shop. Buy relic-pips, hide the store to inspect the board, then place them on your bottom 12 deck pips. Boss and enemy board changes only affect the top half.
