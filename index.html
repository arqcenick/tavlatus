Rules

Pipjack

Score, then survive the blind.

Dice roll automatically. Select a white checker, move by an available die, then End Turn to roll again or cash out a beaten blind.

Base move
Every moved checker scores 10 Chips x die value before Mult.
Scoring
Move Score = Chips x global Mult, then checker and tile Mult can push it higher.
Pass-over hooks
Checkers now jump pip-by-pip. Passed pips can trigger future effects before the checker lands.
Break
Land on one red checker to break it for +200 Chips.
Allied landing
Land on white checkers to gain +50 Chips for each white checker already there.
Bear off
Move past pip 24 to score +250 Chips.
Doubles
Rolling a double turns the matched dice into four moves of that value.
Payout
Clearing a normal blind pays $3, the boss pays $5, and unused rolls pay $1 each.
Mars
Win before a mover enemy reaches the end to double the payout.
Enemies
Bright red enemies stay still. Darker red enemies with swords move 2 pips at end of turn unless blocked by two or more white checkers.
Tiles
Forge gives that checker +1 Mult forever. Market doubles Chips for that move.
Shop
Clear a blind to visit the shop. Buy from two relic offers and three piece upgrade offers before the next blind.
