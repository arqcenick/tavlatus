Rules

Tavlatus

Score, then survive the blind.

Dice roll automatically. Select a white checker, move by an available die, then press Win after beating the target to visit the shop.

Base move
Every moved checker scores 10 Chips x die value before Mult.
Scoring
Move Score = Chips x global Mult, then checker and tile Mult can push it higher.
Pass-over hooks
Checkers now jump pip-by-pip. Passed pips can trigger future effects before the checker lands.
Break
Land on one red checker to break it for +200 Chips.
Allied landing
Land on white checkers to gain +50 Chips for each white checker already there.
Attack
Move past pip 24 to attack and score +250 Chips.
Doubles
Rolling a double turns the matched dice into four moves of that value.
Payout
Clearing a normal blind pays 3 Akçe, the boss pays 5 Akçe, and unused rolls pay 1 Akçe each.
Mars
Win before any moving enemy reaches the end to double the payout.
Enemies
Every enemy phase, the two rearmost enemy pieces AND the two nearest your base each advance by 3 pips (four moves total). Pawns take the largest legal step (up to 3) and are shortened when a defended white gate blocks them. Rams can breach a defended gate and push one white checker to the bar.
Tiles
Relic-pips are placed in your bottom deck. Forge gives that checker +1 Mult forever, Market doubles Chips for that move, and other relic-pips add break, money, haste, or Mult effects.
Shop
Clear a blind to visit the shop. Buy relic-pips, hide the store to inspect the board, then place them on your bottom 12 deck pips. Boss and enemy board changes only affect the top half.
F

The Forge

Landing here temporarily upgrades the checker's rim tier (Basic → Glass → Ruby → Prism) for the round. Passing over it adds +5 Chips to the move.

M

The Market

Landing here doubles the total Chips for this move. Passing over it adds +0.25 Mult to the move.

I

Iron Gate

Breaking a red enemy checker on this pip grants +2 global Mult for the rest of the round.

H

Haste Line

Landing here with a die value of 5 or 6 adds +20 Chips to the move.

$

Ledger Pip

Breaking a red enemy checker on this pip immediately awards +1 Akçe.

+

Dealer Pip

Landing here grants +1 global Mult for the rest of the round.

Basic Core +10 Chips

Standard checker core. Solid, reliable, and represents the backbone of your starting array.

Gold Core +50 Chips

Gilded checker core. Grants a strong baseline boost of +50 Chips to every move made by this checker.

Platinum Core +100 Chips

Lustrous platinum core. Outfitted with royal engravings, granting a massive +100 Chips baseline boost.

Anchor Core +10 Chips

Heavy iron anchor core. Makes the checker invulnerable to enemy capturing/breaking. When defending a gate (even single), enemies are blocked from landing.

Basic Rim x1 Mult

Standard brass rim. Standard multiplier, durable and handles normal movements.

Glass Rim x2 Mult

Fragile, gleaming glass rim. Grants x2 Mult but shatters and breaks permanently if hit by an enemy piece (unless reinforced by an Anchor Core).

Ruby Rim x3 Mult

Polished crimson gemstone rim. Increases the checker's scoring multiplier to x3.

Prism Rim x5 Mult

Legendary color-shifting rim. Yields a massive x5 Mult, unleashing enormous scoring runs.

†

Pawn 3 Pips

Standard enemy checker. Advances up to 3 pips each phase (the move-token value), taking the largest legal distance and shortening its step when a defended player gate (2+ checkers) blocks the way.

R

Ram 3 Pips

Battering Ram enemy. Advances up to 3 pips each phase and can breach a defended player gate when closing at exactly 2 pips, dislodging one player checker to the Bar (or shattering it on a Glass Rim).

W

The Wall Level 3 Boss

Boss mechanism. Restricts the playing area by locking Pips 13 and 14 on the top (enemy) half of the board. Player checkers cannot land on locked pips.

P

The Prime Level 4 Boss

A creeping 6-pip prime wall locks the enemy half. Each enemy phase the entire prime shifts +1 pip toward your deck, then wraps. Your landing space shrinks and moves every turn. Locked pips are marked PRIME on the board.

B

The Backgame Level 5 Boss

Every enemy checker is a Ram — any enemy can breach a defended gate (2+ of your checkers) and dislodge one. Enemies you break also re-enter on the SAME enemy turn instead of waiting. Defense and captures are far less reliable.

C

The Cube Level 6 Boss

The doubling cube raises the stakes: each enemy phase the target score climbs and your global Mult decays (floor x1). Your single highest-value relic-pip is suppressed for the whole fight — its effect is inert and shown crossed-out on the board.
